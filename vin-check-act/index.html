ACT VIN Check – Australian Capital Territory Vehicle Guide

ACT vehicle registration records let you check registration status and some non-identifying vehicle details by number plate. Some registration record requests are restricted to the registered operator or an authorised person.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Important: A VIN decoder is not a registration database. This page links to official Australian Capital Territory services; it does not access government vehicle records itself.

Official Australian Capital Territory registration information

For information about an ACT registration record, Access Canberra says access to detailed records is generally limited to the registered operator, joint operator or an authorised person.

Open official Access Canberra service ↗

Used vehicle checks in ACT

  1. Use the ACT number-plate registration status search for an ACT-registered vehicle.
  2. Do not treat a registration result as proof of ownership, finance status or a complete vehicle history.
  3. Compare the permanent VIN on the vehicle with the sales documents before paying.
  4. Run a separate PPSR search using the VIN or chassis number to check for a recorded security interest and available vehicle history indicators.

VIN check, rego check and PPSR are different

A VIN check reads the identifier. A rego check looks up registration information for a plate or VIN where supported by the state service. A PPSR search is the separate Commonwealth search for security interests and available NEVDIS vehicle indicators. One result does not replace the others.
