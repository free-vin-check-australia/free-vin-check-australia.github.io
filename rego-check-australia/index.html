Australian Rego Check – All States & Territories

Choose the state or territory shown on the number plate and open its official registration lookup. These links take you directly to government services.

Rego check is not a PPSR check. Registration lookups can show registration status and expiry, and some jurisdictions show other vehicle details. They do not replace a PPSR search for finance/security interests. PPSR searches require the VIN or chassis number, not a registration plate.

Before buying a used vehicle

  1. Check the plate using the relevant state or territory service above.
  2. Compare the VIN on the vehicle with its paperwork using our VIN comparison tool.
  3. Run an official PPSR CarCheck using the VIN or chassis number.
  4. Check outstanding safety recalls using the Australian Government recall search.

Why check the state shown on the plate?

Each state and territory maintains its own registration service. A lookup may only return information for vehicles currently or previously registered in that jurisdiction. If the plate is interstate, use the authority responsible for that plate.

Official service links are provided as a convenience. Government websites can change their URLs, login requirements or available information. Always read the terms shown on the official service before relying on a result.
