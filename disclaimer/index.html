Information only

Results are informational and are based on the structure of the VIN. They cannot confirm ownership, finance, damage, registration or odometer history.

Not advice

Nothing on this site is legal, financial or mechanical advice. Run an official PPSR search and arrange an independent inspection before you buy.

Third-party data

Vehicle details come from the NHTSA vPIC database, which is a United States catalogue and may be incomplete or unavailable for Australian-market vehicles. Confirm every detail against the vehicle and its paperwork.

Accuracy

The maker list is a short list of common makers and may not cover every code. Model year codes show the maker's model year, which may differ from the build date.
