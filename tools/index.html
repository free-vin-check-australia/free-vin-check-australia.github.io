Australian Vehicle Check Tools

Simple, free tools and direct links to official Australian vehicle checks. Start with the VIN checker, then use the relevant government service for registration, recalls or vehicle history.

Free VIN check & decoder

Validate a 17-character VIN, view available manufacturer and year-code information, and optionally request extra data from NHTSA vPIC.

Open VIN checker →

Compare two VINs

Compare the VIN on the vehicle with the VIN on paperwork. Find character-by-character differences locally in your browser.

Open VIN comparison →

Australian rego check

Choose a state or territory and open its official registration lookup. No unofficial rego database is used.

Choose your state →

Vehicle recall check

Go to the Australian Government vehicle recall search and learn how to check an outstanding safety recall.

Check recalls →

Official PPSR search

Check a vehicle's security interest and recorded written-off or stolen status through the official PPSR service.

Open PPSR CarCheck →

RAV public search

Look up whether a vehicle has an entry in the Register of Approved Vehicles. This is not a vehicle-history report.

Open RAV search →

Important: These checks are different. A VIN decoder reads the identifier; a rego lookup checks registration; a recall search checks safety notices; and PPSR is the official national search for security interests and recorded stolen or written-off status.
