Privacy policy

How this site handles your information.

Last updated 24 November 2026

VIN numbers

The format check runs in your browser as you type. This site has no server that receives VINs, and it does not save VINs to a database or browser storage.

Third-party decode

When you press Check & Decode VIN, your browser sends the VIN directly to the NHTSA vPIC public API (a United States Government vehicle database) to request vehicle details. NHTSA operates that service under its own policies and rate limits. If you do not want a VIN sent to NHTSA, only type it and read the instant local result without pressing the button, or use the official Australian checks.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies by itself. If you add analytics or advertising, update this policy to describe them.

External links

Links to the PPSR and other sites open their own pages, which have their own privacy policies.
