Privacy policy

How this site handles your information.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Who operates this website

Free VIN Check Australia is an independently operated informational website. The publisher contact details are listed on the Contact Us page. This site is not an Australian Government service and is not affiliated with the PPSR, NEVDIS, NHTSA or state and territory road authorities.

Information entered into the VIN checker

The format checks, character display, local WMI lookup and VIN comparison operate in your browser. The site does not send those local checks to its own application server, does not keep a VIN database and does not use browser local storage to save VINs.

Optional NHTSA vehicle lookup

If you press Check & Decode VIN, the VIN is sent directly from your browser to the public NHTSA vPIC API in the United States to request manufacturer-submitted vehicle data. The request is made only after you choose the lookup button. NHTSA operates its service under its own policies and technical controls. Do not use the optional lookup if you do not want to transmit the VIN to that service. The local format tool remains available without it.

Website hosting and technical logs

This site is designed as a static website and is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other web hosting services, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps and requested files for security, operation and abuse prevention. Refer to GitHub's privacy information for details about its processing. The site owner does not receive a visitor's full VIN from the local-only tools.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current version of this site does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies and does not include an analytics tracker. If analytics, advertising or third-party embeds are added later, this policy must be updated before they are enabled to explain the providers, data collected, cookie or device storage use, consent controls where required, and opt-out choices. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Clipboard features

The Paste and Copy controls use your browser's Clipboard API only when you activate them. Clipboard permissions are controlled by your browser. The site does not store clipboard contents.

Contact messages

If you email the site publisher, the email address and information you choose to include will be used to respond to your message and manage corrections. Please do not send full VINs, identity documents, payment details or other sensitive information unless it is strictly necessary; the site does not need these details for ordinary support.

External websites

Links to government and third-party websites take you away from this site. Those services have their own privacy notices, terms and data handling practices. Check the destination's policy before submitting details.

Children and personal information

This is a general vehicle information tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The website does not ask visitors to create an account.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the site adds analytics, advertising, contact services or new data processing. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest editorial update.

This page explains the current technical operation of the website and is not legal advice. The site owner should review it against the actual hosting, contact, analytics and advertising settings before launch.
