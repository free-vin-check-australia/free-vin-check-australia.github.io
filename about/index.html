About this site

A free tool for checking VIN numbers on Australian vehicles.

Last updated 24 November 2026

What this site does

Free VIN Check Australia validates a VIN, decodes its country, maker and model year code, requests make, model and engine details from the NHTSA vPIC database, and explains how to run the official checks.

What it does not do

It does not hold vehicle records. Finance, write-off and stolen information comes from the PPSR, and registration comes from state road authorities.

Independent

This site is not affiliated with the PPSR, the Australian Government or any state road authority.
