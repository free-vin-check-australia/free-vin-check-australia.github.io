Where to find the VIN on a caravan or trailer

  • On the compliance plate, usually on the drawbar or front of the chassis.
  • Stamped on the chassis rail.
  • On the registration papers.

PPSR check for a caravan

Caravans and trailers can have a security interest recorded against their VIN. Run a search at ppsr.gov.au/carcheck before you pay.

Checks before buying a caravan

  • Match the chassis VIN with the compliance plate and papers.
  • Run a PPSR search.
  • Check the registration status with the state road authority.
  • Inspect the chassis, axle and brakes, and check for water damage.

Frequently asked questions

Do caravans have a VIN?

Yes. Caravans and trailers made for the Australian market carry a VIN or chassis number on the compliance plate.

Can a caravan have money owing?

Yes. A security interest can be recorded against a caravan VIN, and the PPSR search shows it.
