Contact the site publisher

For corrections, broken links, accessibility feedback or questions about how this independent tool works, contact the site editor by email.

Email: Contact details are being configured

Before publishing, add a working public email address in js/site-config.js. This static GitHub Pages website does not have a server-side contact form.

What to include in a message

  • The page URL and a short description of the issue.
  • For a factual correction, include a link to the relevant official Australian source.
  • For a technical problem, include your browser and device type.

Please do not email a full VIN, driver licence, registration certificate, payment details or other sensitive documents. VINs may identify a particular vehicle; send only the minimum information needed to explain a website issue.

Official vehicle records

We cannot amend PPSR, NEVDIS, police or state registration records. For an official record correction, contact the relevant state or territory road authority.
