Used car buying checklist

Follow these checks in order before you pay a deposit on a used car, motorcycle or caravan.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Check a VIN now. Use the free VIN checker to validate a VIN and decode its country, maker and model year.

Before you visit

  1. Ask the seller for the VIN, plate and registration expiry date.
  2. Enter the VIN in the free VIN checker and confirm the country, maker and model year match the advertisement.
  3. Run a PPSR search at ppsr.gov.au/carcheck.
  4. Check the registration status with the state road authority.

At the inspection

  1. Compare the VIN on the windscreen, compliance plate and registration papers.
  2. Look for signs of tampering: new rivets, scratched stamping, mismatched plates.
  3. Ask for the service book and receipts, and compare them with the odometer reading.
  4. Check for modifications, and confirm they are legal.
  5. Take a test drive on different road types.

Independent inspection

Book an independent mechanic. They can check mechanical condition and signs of previous damage or repairs, and spot a tampered VIN or chassis number.

Paperwork and payment

  1. Get a receipt showing the seller's name, address, the VIN and the price.
  2. Complete the transfer with your state road authority within the required time.
  3. Avoid paying cash before the PPSR search and VIN checks are done.
  4. Check for open safety recalls, including Takata airbags.

Walk away if

  • The VINs do not match.
  • The PPSR search shows a security interest the seller will not clear.
  • The vehicle is recorded as stolen or the seller cannot explain a write-off.
  • The seller refuses an independent inspection.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a PPSR check when buying from a dealer?

It is still sensible. A search before payment confirms the record independently.

What should I do if the PPSR shows finance owing?

Ask the seller to have the lender remove the interest before you pay, or walk away.

Is a free VIN check enough?

No. It only validates the code. Use the PPSR search and an inspection as well.
