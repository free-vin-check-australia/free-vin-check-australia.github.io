Australian Vehicle Recall Check

Check whether a car, motorcycle, caravan, trailer or vehicle component may be affected by a safety recall using the Australian Government's official recall service.

Search official vehicle recalls

The government recall database lets you search published vehicle and component recall notices. Search by make, model or recall details on the official website.

Open Australian Vehicle Recalls ↗

How to check a vehicle

  1. Find the make, model and model year from the vehicle documents or compliance plate.
  2. Open the official recall website and search for the make and model.
  3. Read the recall notice carefully, including affected model range, build dates and VIN ranges where listed.
  4. Contact the manufacturer or an authorised dealer to confirm whether your specific VIN is affected and arrange the repair.

A general make/model search is not a VIN-specific clearance. A recall notice may cover only certain build periods or VIN ranges. Confirm your exact vehicle with the manufacturer or dealer.

What happens if a vehicle is recalled?

Follow the instructions in the official recall notice and contact the manufacturer. Recall repairs are generally arranged through the supplier or an authorised repairer; confirm the process and any applicable arrangements directly with them.

Recall check vs PPSR vs VIN decoder

  • VIN decoder: reads identifier information encoded in the VIN.
  • Recall search: finds published safety recall notices; confirm VIN applicability with the manufacturer.
  • PPSR search: checks registered security interests and available recorded written-off or stolen status.

Official source: Australian Government Vehicle Recalls. You can also read the Department of Infrastructure recall guidance.
