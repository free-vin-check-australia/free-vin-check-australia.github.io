TAS VIN Check – Tasmania Vehicle Guide

Tasmania has an important extra step for used-car buyers: the state registration lookup can provide stolen and current written-off status information that may not appear in the PPSR vehicle data feed for Tasmanian vehicles.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Important: A VIN decoder is not a registration database. This page links to official Tasmania services; it does not access government vehicle records itself.

Official Tasmania registration information

The PPSR states that stolen vehicle information is not available on the PPSR for Tasmanian vehicles. Transport Tasmania’s own buyer guidance says to use its registration lookup to check stolen or wrecked status.

Open official Transport Services Tasmania service ↗

Used vehicle checks in TAS

  1. Use the Tasmanian online registration lookup with the vehicle registration number.
  2. Transport Services advises buyers to check whether the vehicle is listed as stolen or wrecked using its online rego check service or by contacting the department.
  3. Run a separate PPSR search with the VIN or chassis number for security interests.
  4. Compare the vehicle’s permanent VIN with its compliance plate and paperwork, and arrange an independent inspection.

VIN check, rego check and PPSR are different

A VIN check reads the identifier. A rego check looks up registration information for a plate or VIN where supported by the state service. A PPSR search is the separate Commonwealth search for security interests and available NEVDIS vehicle indicators. One result does not replace the others.
