Compare Two VIN Numbers

Compare the VIN stamped on a vehicle with the VIN printed on its registration papers, invoice or service documents. This tool runs locally in your browser and does not send either VIN to a server.

Enter both VINs to compare them.

What the result means

A match means the two entered strings are identical after removing spaces and hyphens and converting letters to uppercase. It does not prove that either VIN is genuine or that the documents belong to the vehicle.

If the VINs differ, do not assume it is a simple typo. Re-check every character on the permanent VIN marking, compliance plate and documents. If the mismatch remains, contact the relevant registration authority or a qualified vehicle inspector before buying or transferring the vehicle.

Privacy: This comparison is performed in your browser. The VINs are not submitted to NHTSA or any external service by this page.
