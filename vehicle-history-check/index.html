Vehicle history check in Australia

A car history check combines official records with other data. This guide explains what each source covers and what to check yourself.

Last updated 24 November 2026

What is a vehicle history check?

A vehicle history check, also called a car history check or vehicle history report, collects records linked to one VIN. The core of most reports in Australia is the PPSR search, and some providers add registration details, odometer readings, sales listings and recall data.

What a history report can include

  • Finance check. Whether a security interest is recorded against the VIN, from the PPSR.
  • Written-off record. Whether the vehicle is recorded as written off, and in some reports the type of damage.
  • Stolen record. Whether the VIN or plate is recorded as stolen.
  • Registration details. State, plate and registration expiry date.
  • Vehicle description. Year, make, model, body type and compliance plate date.
  • Odometer and sales history. Included by some providers only.
  • Recall status. Some providers flag recalls such as Takata airbags.

Free VIN check versus history report

A free VIN check validates the code and decodes its structure. A history report looks up databases. Free tools use public data, while paid reports draw on paid databases, which is why they cost money. Start with the free VIN checker, then decide whether you need more than a PPSR search.

How to choose a provider

  • Check that the report includes an official PPSR search certificate.
  • Compare the price with the official PPSR fee.
  • Read what data is actually included and which states are covered.
  • Check the privacy policy before you enter personal details.

What no report can replace

No database knows everything. Unreported damage, poor repairs and a wound-back odometer may not appear. Combine the report with the service book, an independent mechanic's inspection and a test drive. See the used car buying checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is a vehicle history check the same as a PPSR check?

A PPSR check is one part. A history report can add registration, odometer and sales data.

Do I need a paid report if I do a PPSR check?

The PPSR covers finance, written-off and stolen status. Add a paid report if you also want odometer, sales or other history, and always get an inspection.

Can a car history check show accidents?

Only recorded events such as write-offs. Unreported damage will not show.
