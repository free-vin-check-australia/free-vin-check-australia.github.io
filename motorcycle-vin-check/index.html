Motorcycle VIN check

Motorbikes have VINs too. Check the code, find it on the frame and run a PPSR search before you buy.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Check a VIN now. Use the free VIN checker to validate a VIN and decode its country, maker and model year.

Free motorbike VIN check

Use the free VIN checker to validate a motorcycle VIN. It works for motorcycles, scooters and dirt bikes with a 17-character VIN. The maker code in the first three characters often names the bike maker, such as JKA for Kawasaki, JYA for Yamaha and JS1 for Suzuki.

Where to find a motorcycle VIN

  • Stamped on the steering head tube at the front of the frame.
  • On a compliance plate fixed to the frame.
  • On the registration papers and insurance documents.

PPSR check on a motorbike

A PPSR search works for motorcycles using the VIN or chassis number, and shows whether a security interest is recorded and whether the vehicle is recorded as written off or stolen. Run it at ppsr.gov.au/carcheck.

Dirt bikes and off-road bikes

Some off-road bikes have no compliance plate or a non-standard number. Check that the frame stamping matches the paperwork, and treat any bike without a verifiable number with caution.

Checks before buying a bike

  • Match the frame VIN with the registration papers.
  • Run a PPSR search.
  • Look for frame damage and non-standard welds.
  • Inspect service records and have a mechanic look it over.

Frequently asked questions

Do motorcycles need a PPSR check?

Yes, a bike can have finance owing like any vehicle. The PPSR search uses the VIN or chassis number.

Where is the VIN on a motorbike?

Usually on the steering head tube and on a compliance plate on the frame.

Does this tool work for dirt bikes?

It validates any 17-character VIN. Some off-road bikes use non-standard numbers.
